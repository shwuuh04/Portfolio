Design approach

01

Understand

Map the criteria of the game jam and the skills of our Illustrators and Programmers.

02

Ideate

Explore our options following the theme of the game jam and build a development plan.

03

Implement

Adjust the mechanics to adapt to unexpected issues while staying true to spirit of the original concept.

Challenges

01

Adhering to theme

The judges had a theme in place: tabletop games. I had to adapt to and conceptualize a game in an unfamiliar gameplay style.

02

Time constraints

Given only 2 days to plan and program the entire game, I had to design the basic narrative, game mechanics, and visual aesthetics in roughly 6 hours.

03

3-minute rule

8 hours before judging, the judges announced they will only test our games for 3 minutes. I had to redefine our mechanics to showcase as much as possible within that timeframe.

The outcome

A sample direction for a meal-planning experience that makes the next step easy to see, from choosing a recipe to checking off groceries.
