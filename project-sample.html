Challenges

This sample concept focuses on three common friction points in planning meals for the week.

01

Scattered information

Recipes, meal plans, and grocery lists can end up split across different places.

02

Plans change

A busy week can make a rigid meal plan difficult to keep up with.

03

Unused ingredients

It can be hard to remember what is already at home before shopping again.

The outcome

A sample direction for a meal-planning experience that makes the next step easy to see, from choosing a recipe to checking off groceries.
